2. What data we process

We only process what you choose to share with us when you write to us on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram: your name or username on that platform, your phone number or profile, and the content of your messages.

Information about a pregnancy is health data, a special category of data under Article 9 of the GDPR, and we treat it with extra care.

We will never ask you for your ID card or passport number, your home address or your medical records.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We use your data only to reply to you and to offer you emotional support, information and resources. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Articles 6.1.a and 9.2.a GDPR), which you give when you decide to write to us and which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Who can see your data

Only the members of the Lumine team who answer the conversations, all of whom are bound by a duty of confidentiality.

Your messages travel through the platforms you choose (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram), operated by Meta / WhatsApp, which process data according to their own privacy policies.

You can ask us at any time to:

  • delete it (erasure);
  • restrict its processing;
  • object to its processing;
